About these rates. These are the Minimum Support Prices (MSP, न्यूनतम समर्थन मूल्य) for Minor Forest Produce notified by the Government of India, Ministry of Tribal Affairs, under the PMJVM scheme (Ministry list of MFPs, source tribal.nic.in msplist). Every rate is in rupees per kilogram unless the entry says otherwise. A rate applies in every state unless its entry names the states it is notified for. The final weight is taken at the kendra and payment follows the kendra's verified weight.
